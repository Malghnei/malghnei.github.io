## Why I Built It
I train Taekwondo, and there were stretches when I couldn't make it to a dojo or club. Training alone kept me moving, but there was no one to tell me what I was doing wrong.

Plenty of people with busy schedules run into the same wall. Kata is my answer: a way to keep training on your own without losing the feedback that helps you improve.

## Highlights
- Built with input from martial arts clubs at Western University, including Western Taekwondo.

## Status
Kata is live and in active development, with more features on the way.
